  In the heart of the vast continent of Albion lies the island city of Tiris. It was founded by King Kane, who gave his all to aid the God of Light in the gods' Holy War.
From the gods, King Kane received the Holy Songs, which held mystic power, and he was allowed to sing them to heal the land and people of Albion, laid waste by the Holy War.
"Keep the gods' teachings, and spread them far and wide..."
  King Kane's songs had the power to cast out the evil that nests in people's hearts, and to drive away demons.
To those suffering from illness he gave the will to fight it, and to the dying he granted peace.
And so the people revered the king, and called him "the Song Master."
